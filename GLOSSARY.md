# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform: instructors publish courses and learners buy and take them.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student

### Purchases

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of several places in a course for a team.

**Team Seat**:
One place in a Team Licence. It is Claimed once a Learner redeems it.

**PPP**:
A price lowered for the Learner's country, so courses cost less where purchasing power is lower.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's request to get their money back for a purchase. It starts Open and ends Approved or Declined.

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request waiting for staff to decide.

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request staff accepted. It leads to a Refund and ends the Learner's access to the course.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request staff turned down. No money is returned and access stays.

**Refund**:
Money returned to a Learner for a purchase. It starts Pending and ends Issued or Failed.

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose money has not been sent yet.

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund whose money was sent to the Learner.

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund whose money could not be sent.

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase, including the 14th day, during which a Learner may raise a Refund Request.

**Refundable Amount**:
What the Learner paid for the purchase, after any PPP, never the list price.

**Refundable**:
A purchase is Refundable when it is inside its Refund Window, the Learner is below the course's watch-time and completion limits, it is not a Team Licence with a Claimed Team Seat, and it has no Open Refund Request.
